The shop

We opened in the old shoe-repair shop on Kloof Street because we were tired of boutiques that stop at a size 12.

Every style we stock comes in 6 to 22. Half the rail is made by Cape Town labels, and our tailor works upstairs, so a hem or a taken-in waist is done before your event, not after it.

Come in for a fitting. We’ll tell you honestly what works.
