Questions

Before you build

Straight answers from our site managers. Ask anything else on WhatsApp.

How much does it cost to build a house in Cape Town?

From R14 500 / m² for a standard finish. A 180 m² family home lands near R2.6 million, plus plans and plot.

Do you handle plans and council approval?

Yes. Our architect draws the plans and submits them to the City. Approval takes 8 to 12 weeks.

Can we live at home during a renovation?

Usually, yes. We phase the work room by room and seal off dust. Kitchens take about 3 weeks.

How do payments work?

Five staged payments, each one after a milestone you can see in the Friday photo report.
